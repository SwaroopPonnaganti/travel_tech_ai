Project presentation — the solution

SafeRoute AI is a safety-first AI travel assistant.

1

User enters travel details. Free text or a guided form captures origin, destination, date, travellers and budget.

2

AI assistant understands. A natural-language parser extracts intent, resolves city names and detects priority (safe/cheap/fast).

3

Travel planner. A route graph is searched (direct + one-stop) and ranked by fare, duration and a Safety Score.

4

Delay & cancellation. A disrupted leg is removed and the graph is re-searched for alternatives with rebooking guidance.

5

Booking consent. The app asks permission and shows exactly what will be booked before any simulated confirmation.

6

Demo. Plan → book → disrupt → recover → SOS, all offline with no external services.
